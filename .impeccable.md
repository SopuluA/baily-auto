# Design Context — Baily Auto Studio

## Target Audience
Local Columbus OH residents needing car care — working class Westside community. Mobile-first users looking up the shop on their phone. Price-conscious, direct communicators.

## Brand Personality
Street-credible, confident, champion mindset. Tagline: "Take Chances… Make Champions!!!" Not corporate — local, human, proud. The aesthetic should feel like a well-run hustle, not a sanitized franchise.

## Brand Colors
- Accent: Electric blue `#00AAFF`
- Secondary: Silver/chrome `#C0C0C0`
- Background: Near-black `#090909`

## Tone
Bold, direct, no fluff. Community-rooted. The brand is two entities in one — Baily's Turbowash 2 (the walk-in location) and Baily Auto Studio (the detail brand on social).

## Use Cases
1. Walk-in customer confirming hours / location
2. Mobile detail booking
3. Price checking before calling
4. Instagram-referred visitor wanting to see more work

## What Makes It Unforgettable
The personality — "Take Chances… Make Champions!!!" It's not a generic car wash. The gallery of real before/after photos on Instagram is the social proof engine.
