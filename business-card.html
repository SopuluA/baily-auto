Baily Auto Studio — Business Cards

How to print: File → Print → set paper to Letter, margins to None / Minimum, scale to 100%. Print on cardstock (80–100 lb recommended). Cut along the card edges. Each sheet holds 10 cards — 5 fronts or 5 backs. Print fronts first, then flip the sheet and print backs.
